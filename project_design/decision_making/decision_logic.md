# Decision Logic

## Decision Making Process

### Input Sources
1. ML Model Predictions
   - Price predictions
   - Demand forecasts
   - Confidence scores

2. Optimization Results
   - Optimized prices
   - Resource allocations
   - Constraint satisfaction

3. Business Rules
   - Profit margins
   - Market position
   - Risk tolerance
   - Compliance requirements

### Decision Workflow

#### Initial Assessment
1. Data Validation
   - Input completeness
   - Data quality checks
   - Consistency verification

2. Risk Evaluation
   - Market risk
   - Operational risk
   - Compliance risk
   - Financial risk

#### Decision Rules

1. Automatic Approval
   - Within normal price range
   - High confidence score
   - Low risk assessment
   - Clear optimization benefit

2. Manual Review Required
   - Large price changes
   - Low confidence score
   - High risk assessment
   - Complex market conditions

3. Rejection Criteria
   - Below minimum margin
   - Exceeds maximum risk
   - Regulatory violations
   - Technical constraints
