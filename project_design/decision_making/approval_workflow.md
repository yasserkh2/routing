# Approval Workflow

## Workflow Stages

### 1. Initial Processing
- Data validation
- Completeness check
- Format verification
- Business rule validation

### 2. Automated Analysis
- ML prediction review
- Optimization result validation
- Risk assessment
- Compliance check

### 3. Decision Classification

#### Automatic Approval
- Criteria:
  * Within standard deviation
  * High confidence score
  * Low risk assessment
  * Clear business benefit

#### Manual Review Required
- Triggers:
  * Significant price changes
  * Low confidence predictions
  * High risk scenarios
  * Complex market conditions

#### Immediate Rejection
- Conditions:
  * Below minimum margins
  * Regulatory violations
  * Technical constraints
  * Risk threshold exceeded

### 4. Approval Routing

#### Approval Levels
1. System Level
   - Automated checks
   - Rule validation
   - Initial filtering

2. Operations Level
   - Team lead review
   - Technical validation
   - Performance impact

3. Business Level
   - Manager approval
   - Strategic alignment
   - Market impact

4. Executive Level
   - High-value decisions
   - Strategic changes
   - Policy exceptions
